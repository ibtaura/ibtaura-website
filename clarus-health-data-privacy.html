Clarus Health Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Adarna Inc ("Adarna", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares consumer health data through Clarus Health ("Clarus"), and the rights you have over it. It is written for Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law, Connecticut's consumer health data rules and similar state laws, and it applies to everyone who uses Clarus. It adds to our Privacy Policy (https://adarna.us/clarus-privacy), which covers all other information.

The consumer health data we collect

  • Apple Health daily summaries (only with your permission):
    • sleep (time in bed and asleep, sleep stages, time awake);
    • heart and breathing (heart rate, resting and walking heart rate, heart rate variability, heart rate recovery, blood oxygen, breathing rate, blood pressure, breathing disturbances and wrist temperature during sleep);
    • activity and exercise (steps, distances, flights climbed, energy, exercise, move and stand time, VO2 max);
    • walking and mobility (walking speed, steadiness and similar measures, and falls);
    • body measurements (weight, height, body mass index, body fat, lean body mass);
    • nutrition you log (energy, carbohydrate, protein, fat);
    • mindful minutes, handwashing, time in daylight, sound levels and UV exposure.
  • Your check-in answers: how you slept, alcohol, drowsy medicine, feeling unwell, stress, how recovered and how tired you feel, and the moods you log.
  • Quick check results: reaction speed and accuracy, switching between rules, and memory span.
  • Weight, if you choose to give it during setup.
  • What Clarus works out from these: your capacity, attention, thinking, memory, recovery and burnout risk scores, and your sleep debt.

Where it comes from

  • From you, when you answer check-ins, take the Quick check, log moods or fill in your setup.
  • From your iPhone's Apple Health, only with your permission. The raw readings stay on your iPhone: Clarus works out daily summaries on the phone, and saves them to your account only if you allow it.

Why we collect and use it

Only to provide the features you ask for: to work out your scores, plan your day around your energy, show your history on your iPhone and Mac, and keep the service secure. We do not use consumer health data for advertising or marketing, and we never sell it.

Your consent

We collect consumer health data only with your consent. You choose whether to connect Apple Health, which kinds of data Clarus may read, whether to save daily summaries to your account, and whether to answer check-ins. You can withdraw your consent at any time: turn off Clarus's access in Apple Health, choose "Not Now" for saving summaries, stop answering check-ins, or delete your account. We would ask for your separate consent before sharing consumer health data for any purpose not described here.

Who we share it with

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS), our processor, stores and processes it for us in the United States, only to provide Clarus, under a contract that limits how it may use the data.
  • We do not share consumer health data with any other third party or affiliate, and we do not sell it.
  • We may disclose it where the law requires, for example in response to a valid court order.

Apple and Google receive sign-in information when you sign in with them. They receive no consumer health data from us.

We do not use geofences, around health care facilities or anywhere else.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data;
  • get a copy of it, including a list of every third party and affiliate we have shared it with and how to contact them;
  • withdraw your consent to our collecting and sharing it;
  • have it deleted, from our systems and from our processor's;
  • appeal if we turn down a request.

How to use your rights

  • Delete: in the app, on iPhone, Settings › Account › Delete Account; on Mac, Settings › Account & Cloud › Delete Account. This deletes your account and all its consumer health data straight away; copies in our encrypted backups are overwritten within 35 days.
  • Everything else: email admin@adarna.us from the address on your account, or write to us at the address below.

We will confirm your identity and answer within 45 days. If we need more time (up to another 45 days), we will tell you why. Requests are free of charge.

Appeals. If we turn down your request, reply to our answer to appeal. We will respond within 45 days and explain our decision. If you're not satisfied with the outcome, you can contact your state's Attorney General: in Washington at atg.wa.gov, in Nevada at ag.nv.gov, and in Connecticut at portal.ct.gov/ag.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the app before a change that affects how we use your consumer health data.
