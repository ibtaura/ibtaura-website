# SEO ranking plan: keywords and timelines (drafted 2026-11-18)

The clock starts when the site fixes go live (planned Dec 2026). These are estimates for a new, low-authority domain, not guarantees. Backlinks and press shorten every timeline.

| Tier | Keyword | Target | Time | Unlocks it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Brand | adarna inc | #1 | 2–8 weeks | Organization schema with sameAs, "Adarna Inc." in titles, profiles, sitemap resubmitted |
| 1 Brand | aura clarus | #1 | 1–3 months | Product schema, product page, Kickstarter backlink, first press |
| 1 Brand | aura clarus band / app / desktop / kickstarter | Top 3 | 1–3 months | Matching titles on the existing pages |
| 2 Brand + category | adarna wearable / band / app | Top 3 | 2–4 months | Exact phrases in titles, H1s and descriptions |
| 2 Brand + category | adarna productivity band | Top 3 | 2–5 months | Position the product as a productivity/focus band; the site never says "productivity" today |
| 3 Niche | productivity band, focus band, productivity wearable | Page 1 → top 3 | 6–10 → 12–16 months | Landing and comparison pages, 15–30 backlinks, inclusion in roundups. Competitors: Pylot, FOCI, Neurable |
| 3 Niche | wearable for deep work, burnout tracker | Page 1 | 6–13 months | Use-case pages, articles that link to the product |
| 3 Niche | Health Library long-tail | Page 1 for some | 3–10 months | Named authors and a medical reviewer (YMYL), dates, internal links, 2–4 articles a month |
| 4 Hard | adarna | Page 1 US → #1 | 9–16 months → 2–3+ years | Knowledge Panel, press, growing brand searches; the legend and Adarna House dominate |
| 4 Hard | wearables, stress tracker, fitness band | Page 1 | 2–3+ years | Don't chase; get listed in the roundups that already rank instead |

## Phases
1. **Nov–Dec 2026, foundation:** ship the restructure and CSS work, a 404 page and headers; Organization schema; brand titles; Cloudflare HTTPS and www; Search Console and Bing.
2. **Dec 2026–Feb 2027, positioning:** agree the product wording; productivity/focus landing, comparison and use-case pages; Product and FAQ schema; image speed; authors and dates on articles.
3. **Feb–May 2027, authority:** consistent profiles, launch press release, directories, roundup and podcast pitches, guest articles, Wikidata.
4. **Jun–Nov 2027, scale:** 2–4 articles a month, creator reviews, refresh pages ranked 5–20.
5. **2028, head terms:** push plain "Adarna" (US) and broad wearable terms.

**KPIs (Search Console, monthly):** position for each keyword, branded vs non-branded impressions and clicks, referring domains (goal 15–30 by mid-2027), all 98 public pages indexed.
