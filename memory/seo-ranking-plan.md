# SEO ranking plan: keywords and timelines (updated 2026-11-18)

The product is now **Clarus Health** (renamed from Aura Clarus). Positioning: a health wearable that helps with productivity and burnout. The clock starts at the first go-live (Release 1). These are estimates for a new, low-authority domain, not guarantees; backlinks and press shorten every timeline.

| Tier | Keyword | Target | Time | Unlocks it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Brand | adarna inc | #1 | 2–8 weeks | Organization schema with sameAs (LinkedIn, Crunchbase, GitHub), "Adarna Inc." in titles, sitemap resubmitted |
| 1 Brand | clarus health adarna · clarus health band · clarus health wearable | #1 / top 3 | 1–3 months | `/clarus-health` page and Product schema, "Clarus Health by Adarna Inc." titles, Kickstarter backlink |
| 1 Brand | clarus health (alone) | Top 3 | 4–9 months | Shared with a San Francisco clinic and a 2025 trademark filing; needs backlinks using the name, and a trademark check |
| 2 Brand + category | adarna wearable / adarna health band | Top 3 | 2–4 months | Exact phrases in titles, H1s and descriptions |
| 2 Brand + category | adarna productivity band | Top 3 | 2–5 months | The `/focus` page and positioning copy |
| 3 Niche | burnout wearable / tracker · productivity wearable · focus band | Page 1 → top 3 | 6–10 → 12–16 months | `/burnout` and `/focus` pages, a comparison page, 15–30 backlinks, inclusion in roundups. Competitors: Pylot, FOCI, Neurable |
| 3 Niche | Health Library long-tail | Page 1 for some | 3–10 months | Named authors and a health reviewer (YMYL), dates, internal links, 2–4 articles a month |
| 4 Hard | adarna | Page 1 US → #1 | 9–16 months → 2–3+ years | Knowledge Panel, press, growing brand searches; the legend and Adarna House dominate |
| 4 Hard | wearables, stress tracker, fitness band | Page 1 | 2–3+ years | Don't chase directly; get listed in the roundups that already rank |

## Releases
1. **Release 1, week 1:** rename to Clarus Health with 301s; one Organization entity; Product schema; "| Adarna Inc." titles; 404 page; `_headers`; sitemap generated from git; article dates.
2. **Release 2, weeks 2–6:** `/burnout`, `/focus` and comparison pages; internal links; FAQ; authors and reviewers on articles; WebP images; defer Lenis.
3. **Release 3 onward, month 2+:** 2–4 articles a month; refresh pages ranked 5–20.

Each release goes live only after the owner's OK. The team's steps (security, trademark check, Cloudflare, Search Console, profiles, press) are in the approved plan and are tracked in [changelog.md](changelog.md).

**KPIs (Search Console, monthly):** position for each keyword, branded vs non-branded clicks, referring domains (goal 15–30 by mid-2027), all public pages indexed.
