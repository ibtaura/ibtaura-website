Clarus Health Privacy Policy

Adarna Inc ("Adarna", "we", "us", "our") makes Clarus Health ("Clarus"), an app for iPhone and Mac that helps you understand your mental energy and plan your day around it. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Clarus, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

Clarus is for adults aged 18 and over in the United States.

The short version

  • We collect only what Clarus needs to work out your scores, plan your day and keep your iPhone and Mac in step.
  • Raw Apple Health data stays on your iPhone. Only daily summaries reach our servers, and only if you allow it.
  • We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Your health information is never used for marketing or advertising.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time in the app, or ask us for a copy of your data.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account. You sign in with Apple or Google. From Google we receive your name and email address. From Apple we receive your email address (or a private relay address, if you choose "Hide My Email") and, the first time you sign in, your name if you choose to share it. We also create an account ID.
  • Setup and preferences. Your work days and hours, lunch time, usual bedtime and wake time, how much sleep you need, your weight if you choose to give it, your time zone, and your appearance and reminder choices.
  • Check-ins. Your answers to the morning check-in (for example how you slept, alcohol, drowsy medicine, feeling unwell, stress and how recovered you feel), the weekly check-in and the evening switch-off, and the moods you log.
  • To complete answers. How draining a meeting, focus block or task was, and how a break went.
  • Quick check results. Your results from the short brain check: reaction speed and accuracy, switching between rules, and how many items you remember in a row.
  • Tasks and plans. The tasks, lists, calendar events and notes you create in Clarus, and what happened to them.
  • Problem reports. If you use Report a Problem: what you write, a screenshot if you attach one, and, only if you turn it on, your device model and system version.
  • Messages to us. If you email us, your email address and what you write.

Information from Apple Health (iPhone only, with your permission)

If you connect Apple Health, Clarus reads only the kinds of data it uses in your scores and on its Search page:

  • Sleep: time in bed and asleep, sleep stages (deep, core and REM), time awake and awakenings.
  • Heart and breathing: heart rate, resting and walking heart rate, heart rate variability, heart rate recovery, blood oxygen, breathing rate, blood pressure, and breathing disturbances and wrist temperature during sleep.
  • Activity and exercise: steps, distances walked, run, cycled, swum or skied, flights climbed, active and resting energy, exercise and move minutes, stand hours, running and cycling measures, and VO2 max.
  • Walking and mobility: walking speed, step length, walking steadiness and similar measures, and falls.
  • Body: weight, height, body mass index, body fat and lean body mass.
  • Nutrition: the energy, carbohydrate, protein and fat you log in Apple Health.
  • Mindfulness and habits: mindful minutes and handwashing.
  • Surroundings: time in daylight, environmental and headphone sound levels, and UV exposure.

Clarus writes one kind of data to Apple Health: the moods you log, saved as State of Mind.

How Apple Health data is handled:

  • The raw readings stay on your iPhone. Clarus works out daily summaries on your iPhone, such as total sleep, average resting heart rate and daily steps.
  • Daily summaries and last night's sleep are saved to your Clarus account only after you allow it in the app ("Keep Your Health Data in Your Clarus Account?"), so your Mac can show them and your scores can use them.
  • You can change what Clarus reads at any time in the Health app (your profile › Apps › Clarus) or in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Clarus.
  • We never use Apple Health data for marketing or advertising, never sell it, never share it with anyone for their own purposes, and never store it in iCloud.

Information collected automatically

  • App basics. Each request from the app carries the app's name and version and your time zone, so our service can answer correctly.
  • Your Mac. The Mac app registers with an ID it creates for that Mac and the Mac's name, so your devices stay in step.
  • Service logs. Our servers keep short technical records (time, request and result) to keep Clarus working and secure.

We don't collect your precise location, contacts, photos (other than a screenshot you choose to attach), browsing history or advertising identifiers. Clarus has no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple, and we never see your card or payment details. Apple tells us whether your subscription is in its free trial or active, its renewal and expiry dates and its transaction IDs, so we can unlock Clarus on your iPhone and Mac. If you own a Clarus Band, we record that it's linked to your account, so you can use Clarus without a subscription.

Age (some states)

In states whose laws require age checks for apps (today Texas, Utah and Louisiana), Apple may tell Clarus your age range. We use it only to keep Clarus for adults, as those laws require.

How we use information

We use your information only to:

  • provide Clarus: work out your capacity, attention, thinking, memory, recovery and burnout risk scores and your sleep debt, plan your day, show your history, and keep your iPhone and Mac in step;
  • remind you of events and check-ins (reminders are scheduled on your own device);
  • unlock your subscription or Clarus Band features;
  • answer your messages and fix the problems you report;
  • keep Clarus secure, prevent misuse, and meet our legal obligations.

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't build profiles about you for anyone else. Clarus doesn't make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. Clarus is a wellness app, not a medical device.

Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising.

We use these service providers. Our contracts let them use your information only to provide their service to us:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) runs our servers and stores your Clarus account data in the United States.
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store subscriptions) and Google (Google sign-in) confirm who you are when you sign in with them, and Apple handles subscription payments. They receive no health data from us.

We may also disclose information:

  • when the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order;
  • to protect the safety, rights or property of our users, the public or Adarna;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. This policy keeps protecting your information, and we will tell you before it becomes subject to a different policy.

How long we keep information

  • Daily Apple Health summaries: 30 days, then they are deleted automatically.
  • Everything else in your account (setup, check-ins, scores, tasks and plans): while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account: we delete the account and its data from all our services straight away. Copies in our encrypted backups are overwritten within 35 days.
  • Problem reports and emails: as long as we need them to help you and fix the problem.

We keep information longer only where the law requires it.

Security

Your data is encrypted while it travels between your devices and our servers (HTTPS) and while it's stored. Access is limited to the parts of our service, and the people, that need it. Your sign-in is kept in your device's secure Keychain. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal or health information, we will tell you as the law requires.

Your choices

  • Apple Health: choose what Clarus reads in the Health app, or don't connect it. Choose "Not Now" if you don't want daily summaries saved to your account.
  • Reminders: Settings › Notifications in Clarus, or your device's notification settings.
  • Sign out: Settings › Account › Sign Out.
  • Delete your account: on iPhone, Settings › Account › Delete Account; on Mac, Settings › Account & Cloud › Delete Account. This deletes your account and its data on every device. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription with Apple; cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
  • A copy of your data: email admin@adarna.us.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we collect, use and disclose;
  • get a copy of your personal information;
  • correct inaccurate personal information;
  • delete your personal information;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising (we do neither);
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information (we already use it only to provide Clarus);
  • not be treated differently for using these rights.

To use these rights, email admin@adarna.us from the address on your account, or use the in-app options above. We will confirm your identity and answer within 45 days, and if we need more time (up to another 45 days) we will tell you why. You can use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof that they act for you. If we turn down your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and we will respond within 45 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact your state's Attorney General.

California residents. In the last 12 months we collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (name, email address, account ID, device ID); sensitive personal information (health information, and your account sign-in through Apple or Google); internet or other electronic network activity (app version and technical logs); and inferences (your scores). We collect them from you, from your devices and Apple Health, and from Apple and Google when you sign in with them. We use them for the purposes in "How we use information" and disclose them only to the service providers listed above. We do not sell or share personal information, and we have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the personal information of anyone under 16. We don't share personal information with third parties for their direct marketing.

Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and similar laws). See our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy: https://adarna.us/clarus-health-data-privacy

Children

Clarus is not for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, email admin@adarna.us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change matters for how we use your information, we will tell you in the app and, where the law requires, ask for your consent first.
