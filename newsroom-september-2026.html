Company & Funding

This month the company took on a new identity and a new home: IBT Aura is now Adarna Inc., registered as a Delaware C-Corporation and set up as a separate entity, giving us the corporate structure that investors, partners and customers in the United States expect from day one. Alongside that change, we and our venture partner, MGM Investments, have decided to part ways because our visions for the company differ, and the termination of that arrangement is now in process, a decision taken deliberately so that the direction of the company stays clear and unambiguous. To keep momentum through the transition, we raised $5,000 in family investment, a modest but meaningful commitment that funds the immediate work on hardware, manufacturing and the launch ahead. Taken together, these steps leave Adarna with a cleaner structure, a single shared vision and the capital to keep building, and the full details are set out in the company update available to download here.

Aluminium Frame

The physical form of Aura Clarus moved from design into production this month, as the three-part aluminium frame entered the manufacturing process. The frame is the structural heart of the device, the part that holds the electronics, the glass and the band together and gives the product its feel on the wrist, so committing it to metal is one of the most important steps in turning a finished design into a real object. Making the frame as three separate parts lets each piece be produced to tight tolerances and then brought together into a single, rigid housing for the device. We expect the finished frames to arrive in late October 2026, and once they are in hand they will let us assemble and test complete devices for the first time, moving Aura Clarus another firm step closer to the people who will wear it.

Optical Glass

Alongside the aluminium frame, the optical glass frame for Aura Clarus has also entered the manufacturing process this month. The glass is far more than a cover: it sits between the device's optical sensors and the skin, so its clarity and fit directly shape how cleanly the sensors can read the signals that the whole product depends on. Producing it in parallel with the aluminium frame keeps the hardware schedule tight, so the two halves of the enclosure progress together and arrive close to one another rather than one waiting on the other. We expect the optical glass frames in early November 2026, at which point the frame, the glass and the electronics can come together into complete Aura Clarus units, ready for assembly and testing.

Website

With the company now operating as Adarna Inc., our website has moved with it, from ibtaura.com to its new address at adarna.us. Everything that lived on the old site has come across, from the Aura Clarus app and desktop pages to the articles, the newsroom, the data room and our policies, so visitors, investors and early users will find the same content under a name that now matches the company behind it. Bringing the domain in line with the company name makes Adarna simpler to find, easier to remember and more credible to anyone meeting us for the first time, whether they arrive from a search, a link or a conversation. From here on, adarna.us is the single official home for Adarna and Aura Clarus, and every future update, launch and announcement will be published there first.

Kickstarter

Our plans for bringing Aura Clarus to its first customers took a concrete step forward this month, as the Kickstarter campaign documents were published on our website. Putting them online in advance means anyone interested in backing the product can read about the campaign, understand what we are building and see how the launch is planned before it opens, rather than meeting all of it for the first time on launch day. Kickstarter is a natural first stage for a product like Aura Clarus, letting the people who believe in it earliest support it directly while giving us real demand to plan manufacturing around. The campaign itself is planned for early December, and until then the documents on our Kickstarter page are the best place to follow what is coming and how to take part.

Aura Clarus 3D Model

The complete 3D model of Aura Clarus is now ready, bringing every part of the product together into a single, detailed digital design for the first time. The model captures the device as a whole, from the outer form a person will see and hold to the way the internal parts sit and fit together, giving us one accurate reference for how Aura Clarus looks and how it is built. Having that unified model matters well beyond the visuals, because manufacturing, assembly and the product imagery on our website and campaign materials are all drawn from it, so everyone working on the product is building from the same picture. With the 3D model finished and the aluminium and glass frames already in production, the design of Aura Clarus is settled and the work is now firmly focused on turning it into real, finished devices.
