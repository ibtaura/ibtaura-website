3. Accuracy of Measurements

Clarus Health estimates its metrics from wrist-worn sensors. Wrist-based measurement is inherently approximate and is affected by fit, movement, skin characteristics, temperature and individual physiology.

Readings are best understood as trends over time for one person — you, compared with your own baseline — and not as precise absolute values, nor as comparable with clinical instruments. No metric produced by Clarus Health has been clinically validated.

4. Emergencies

Clarus Health is not an emergency device. It does not detect medical emergencies, does not call for help, and must never be relied upon for that purpose. In an emergency in the United States, call 911, or contact your local emergency services.

5. Who Should Take Extra Care
