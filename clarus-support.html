Report a problem from the app

In Clarus, open Settings and choose Report a Problem. Say what happened and, if it helps, attach a screenshot. Your device model and system version are included only if you turn that on.

Common questions

Signing in

How do I sign in? With your Apple or Google account. Use the same one on your iPhone and your Mac, and your scores, tasks and plans stay in step on both.

I used to sign in with my email address. Clarus no longer uses email codes. Sign in with Google using the same email address, and your account opens as before.

I chose "Hide My Email" with Apple. That's fine. Your account uses Apple's private relay address, and you sign in with Apple each time.

Your subscription

What does Clarus cost? New subscribers start with a 1-month free trial. After it, Clarus costs US$15 a month, billed by Apple. One subscription works on your iPhone and your Mac. If you own a Clarus Band, you can use Clarus without a subscription.

How do I avoid being charged after the free trial? Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, in your Apple Account settings (see below). You keep the rest of the trial.

I have an offer code. On the subscription screen in Clarus, choose Redeem Code, or redeem it in the App Store.

How do I cancel? On iPhone: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Clarus Health. On Mac: App Store › [your name] › Account Settings › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date; you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting your Clarus account doesn't cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed, but Clarus doesn't know. In Clarus, choose Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Scores and check-ins

Why is there no morning score today? Your morning score starts when you answer the morning check-in. It stays on Today until you answer it, through the end of the day.

How accurate are the scores? They're worked out from your answers, your Quick check and your Apple Health data, and measured against your own normal, so they get better over your first two weeks. Clarus is a wellness app, not medical advice.

Apple Health

Clarus isn't reading my sleep or heart rate. Open the Health app, tap your profile, then Apps › Clarus, and turn on the data you want Clarus to read (or go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Clarus). For sleep data, wear your Apple Watch overnight or use another sleep source that writes to Apple Health.

What does Clarus save? Raw Health readings stay on your iPhone. If you allow it, Clarus saves daily summaries to your account so your Mac can show them; they are kept for 30 days.

Reminders

I'm not getting reminders.

  • iPhone: Settings › Notifications › Clarus › Allow Notifications.
  • Mac: System Settings › Notifications › Clarus.
  • In Clarus: Settings › Notifications › Event reminders lets you choose All, Only selected or None.

Clarus Band

What does the Clarus Band add? Continuous readings that make your scores more accurate, and the Recovery and Work modes that run with it. Learn more at https://adarna.us/clarus-band

Your data and account

How do I delete my account? On iPhone: Settings › Account › Delete Account. On Mac: Settings › Account & Cloud › Delete Account. This deletes your account and its data on every device.

How do I get a copy of my data? Email admin@adarna.us from the address on your account.
