Aura Clarus Desktop — Your Health Command Center for macOS

Dashboard

Your morning command center. One screen shows your body's readiness badge, today's AI-generated health alerts pulled from your wearable data, live vitals including recovery, HRV, and sleep, plus your priority tasks for the day. This is the only screen that shows your health and your work side by side — because your energy level determines what kind of work you should do today.

Focus

A complete deep work environment built for founders who know willpower alone isn't enough. One tap starts your timer, blocks distracting websites at the OS level across every browser, plays focus music, and silences all notifications. When you're done, you see exactly how focused you actually were — context switches, refocus time, and minutes in true flow state.

Trends

Your personal health analytics over 30, 45, and 60 days. See patterns in steps, deep sleep, HRV, stress, and recovery with detailed bar charts that reveal which weeks were strong and which weren't. AI-generated insights tell you what the trend actually means — "Your HRV has been declining for 3 weeks" is more useful than just showing today's number.

Calendar

Your entire week, colour-coded by energy type. Meetings in green, deep work in orange, wellness in purple, fitness in red, and personal time in teal — all visible at a glance across day, week, and month views. The colour coding isn't cosmetic — a week full of green with no orange tells you immediately that you have no time for actual work, changing how you plan before the week even starts.

Work

A Kanban board that lives alongside your health data. Three columns — Pending, Currently Working, and Completed — with colour-coded priority badges: red for urgent, orange for high, blue for medium, gray for low. This isn't competing with Linear or Jira. It's the personal task board where health data and work talk to each other — when your Dashboard says recovery is low, this is where you decide which tasks to defer.

Habits

Daily habit tracking deeply integrated with your health wearable. Build streaks with recovery-aware grace days that don't break your chain when your body genuinely needs rest. Over time, see which habits actually move the needle on your HRV and sleep quality — turning guesswork into evidence-backed routines that compound into lasting change.

Eva Assistant

Your AI health companion with an iMessage-style interface. Ask "How's my stress?" and get a real answer backed by your actual wearable data, complete with mini health cards showing sparklines and trends. Not a generic chatbot regurgitating wellness tips — Eva is an assistant that genuinely knows your body, your patterns, and your history, giving answers no other AI can.
