Long boilerplate (100 words)

Adarna Inc. is a Delaware corporation headquartered in San Francisco, California, founded by Vishwanath Kannan (Founder and CEO) and Adithisri Vishwanath (Co-Founder and COO). The company makes Clarus Health, a health wearable that helps with productivity and burnout. The Clarus Health band is worn on the upper arm, clear of the keyboard, and reads heart rhythm and six more signals. The Clarus Health iPhone app estimates burnout risk against the wearer's own 14-day baseline and suggests focus blocks and recovery breaks, while the Clarus Health Desktop app for Mac plans work around daily capacity. Clarus Health launches in July 2027.

Clarus Health

Clarus Health by Adarna Inc. is a health wearable that helps with productivity and burnout. The band sits on the upper arm, clear of the keyboard and trackpad, and reads heart rhythm and six more signals: heart rate, beat-to-beat timing, breathing, skin temperature, blood oxygen, steps and activity. The Clarus Health iPhone app and the Clarus Health Desktop app for Mac turn those readings into a burnout risk estimate, focus blocks and recovery breaks; the burnout estimate is meant to help pace work and is not a diagnosis.

Read more on the Clarus Health product page, about how Clarus Health helps with burnout, and about how Clarus Health supports focus. Launch news is on the Clarus Health Kickstarter page and in the Adarna Newsroom.
